This article answers the questions parents often ask about Casa Preschool's new campus in the Ciputra urban area. The answers are based on information the school has confirmed; anything that is still a plan is clearly marked as planned. The article will be updated when there is new information.

## When will the Ciputra campus open?

The campus is expected to open in Q4 2027. Families who register early will be told the visit schedule once the campus is ready.

## Where is the campus?

On the 2nd and 3rd floors and rooftop of Block 1 in the Ciputra urban area, Phú Thượng ward, Hanoi. The street number and official building name will be added when available.

## From what age do you accept children?

We welcome children from 15 months to 6 years, according to the campus programme and admission conditions. The school will discuss details with each family.

## What programme will my child follow?

- **15 months to 3 years:** mixed-age classes in the spirit of Montessori. Children choose their own activities, learn alongside older children and are introduced to English every day. See the article [What is a mixed-age class?](/en/news/what-is-a-mixed-age-class/).
- **3 to 6 years:** a programme based on the Cambridge Early Years framework for children aged 3–6, covering Early Years 1, 2 and 3. The final year is the pre-primary class, with added skills to prepare for Grade 1. See the [Kindergarten, 3–6 years](/en/programmes/kindergarten/) page.

## Which tracks will the campus offer?

Two tracks are planned for children aged 3–6:

- **International track:** a foreign teacher in every class, alongside a homeroom teacher and a teaching assistant.
- **Bilingual track:** a homeroom teacher and two teaching assistants; a foreign teacher teaches set lessons.

The article [Bilingual or international: which track suits your child?](/en/news/bilingual-or-international-which-track-for-your-child/) helps you compare the two tracks.

## The campus is on upper floors: how do drop-off and safety work?

Parents drop off and pick up children in front of the building lobby, and children go up to class by lift (planned). Safety arrangements for the lifts, classroom floors, rooftop, emergency exits and drop-off area will be published once the campus is complete and fully confirmed.

## What are the school hours and meals?

At Casa's current campuses, children attend from 7:30 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, and have 4–5 meals a day depending on the campus, cooked by the school's own one-way kitchen. The detailed daily schedule at the Ciputra campus will be published once the campus is complete.

## What are the fees?

The fee schedule for the 2027–2028 school year is being finalised. Families who register will be the first to receive it. You can also contact us to receive the fee schedule when it is ready.

## How do I register?

For now, you can register for early updates on the [Casa Preschool – Ciputra](/en/campuses/ciputra/) page, call the hotline on +84 989 059 838 or message us on Zalo at the same number. When the campus opens its visit schedule, admissions are expected to follow the same steps as at our current campuses: advice and booking a visit, visiting the campus, a trial day and enrolment, then the school supports your family through the first weeks. The detailed steps are on the [Admissions](/en/admissions/) page.

## Where can I see the school in the meantime?

You are welcome to visit one of our [three current campuses](/en/campuses/) in Kim Mã, Chùa Láng and Mỹ Đình to see how Casa runs its classes, meals and daily routine.
