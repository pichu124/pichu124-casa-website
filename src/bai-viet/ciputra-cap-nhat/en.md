This article sums up what is confirmed about Casa's new campus in the Ciputra area, what the school will announce in the coming months, and how you can be among the first to hear. It will be updated whenever there is new information.

## What is confirmed

**Location.** The new campus is inside the Ciputra urban area, Phú Thượng ward, Hanoi, on the 2nd and 3rd floors and rooftop of Block 1.

**Ages.** Children from 12 months to 6 years.

**Programmes by age:**

| Age | Programme |
| --- | --- |
| 12 months to 3 years | Mixed-age classes in the spirit of Montessori: children choose activities, learn alongside older children and are introduced to English every day |
| 3–5 years (Early Years 1 and 2) | A programme based on the Cambridge Early Years framework for children aged 3–6: learning through play, developing all six areas |
| 5–6 years (Early Years 3) | Pre-primary class, with added skills to prepare for Grade 1 |

The article [What is a mixed-age class?](/en/news/what-is-a-mixed-age-class/) explains how mixed-age classes work. The article [What is Cambridge Early Years?](/en/news/what-is-cambridge-early-years/) explains the framework for children aged 3–6.

## What is planned

The information below is the school's current plan and may be adjusted as the campus is completed:

- **Timing:** the campus is expected to open in Q4 2027.
- **Two tracks** for children aged 3–6: the international track, with a foreign teacher in every class; the bilingual track, where a foreign teacher teaches set lessons. The comparison table is on the [Programmes](/en/programmes/#so-sanh-he) page.
- **Drop-off and pick-up:** parents drop off and pick up children in front of the building lobby; children go up to class by lift.

## What will be announced

The school will announce the following once everything is confirmed:

- the street number and official building name;
- renders and photos of the campus;
- safety arrangements for the lifts, classroom floors, rooftop, emergency exits and drop-off area;
- the fee schedule for the 2027–2028 school year;
- the campus visit schedule.

## How to get early updates

Families who register on the [Casa Preschool – Ciputra](/en/campuses/ciputra/) page will receive programme information, fees and the visit schedule as soon as they are available. You can also call the hotline on +84 989 059 838, message us on Zalo at the same number, or follow the school's Facebook page.

In the meantime, you are welcome to visit one of our [three current campuses](/en/campuses/) in Kim Mã, Chùa Láng and Mỹ Đình to see how Casa runs its classes, meals and daily routine.
