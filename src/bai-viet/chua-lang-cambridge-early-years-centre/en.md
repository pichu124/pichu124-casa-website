Casa Preschool's Chùa Láng campus has been approved by Cambridge as a Cambridge Early Years Centre, centre number VN068. The certificate was issued by Cambridge University Press & Assessment on 20 July 2026 to Casa dei Bambini Preschool and applies to the Chùa Láng campus. This article explains what the certificate means and how children learn at the Chùa Láng campus.

## What does this certificate mean?

The Chùa Láng campus is an approved Cambridge Early Years Centre, registered to offer the early years programme from the Cambridge Pathway for children aged 3–6. In other words, the campus is registered with Cambridge to teach this programme.

Cambridge Early Years is the first stage of the Cambridge Pathway, which runs from age 3 to 19. According to Cambridge, the programme helps children aged 3–6 develop the knowledge, understanding and skills they need to meet key early milestones and thrive in and outside of school.

## What does the Cambridge Early Years programme include?

As Cambridge describes it, the programme is:

- **Child-centred and play-based.** Children grow at their own pace and connect their development with the world and people around them.
- **Holistic and balanced.** It is divided into three years, Early Years 1, 2 and 3, with six areas of development: communication, language and literacy; creative expression; mathematics; personal, social and emotional development; physical development; and understanding the world.
- **Equipped with what schools need:** classroom resources, approaches to measuring children's progress, and professional development opportunities for teachers.
- **Preparing children for the move to primary school,** with a focus on all-round development.

The article [What is Cambridge Early Years?](/en/news/what-is-cambridge-early-years/) explains the framework in more detail.

## How do children learn at the Chùa Láng campus?

At the Chùa Láng campus, classes for ages 3–6 follow the international track: every class has a foreign teacher, together with a homeroom teacher and a teaching assistant. Children aged 5–6 have a separate pre-primary class, following Early Years 3 with added skills to prepare for Grade 1.

Children from 15 months to 3 years attend nursery classes guided by Montessori principles, getting used to English through songs, games and stories. From age 3 they continue into the kindergarten programme, so they do not need to change schools along the way.

On a day at the Chùa Láng campus, children attend from 7:30 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, and have 5 meals cooked by the school's own one-way kitchen, including fresh milk at 16:00.

See the three kindergarten years and the two tracks on the [Kindergarten, 3–6 years](/en/programmes/kindergarten/) page.

## Visit the Chùa Láng campus

The Chùa Láng campus is on the 1st–2nd floors, Lobby A1, HongKong Tower, 185 Huỳnh Thúc Kháng, Láng ward, Hanoi. You can book a visit of about 30–45 minutes to see the classrooms and talk with the teachers. The address, map and visit booking are on the [Chùa Láng campus](/en/campuses/chua-lang/) page.
